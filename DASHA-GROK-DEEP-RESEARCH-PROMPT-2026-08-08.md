## Evidence rules

- Search current web sources as of 2026-08-08.
- Prefer peer-reviewed papers, conference proceedings, preprints, official product documentation, public repositories, public/on-chain datasets and direct public posts.
- Label evidence as experiment, quasi-experiment, observational, qualitative, theoretical, preprint, official product claim, practitioner observation or anecdote.
- Record dates, sample sizes when known, limitations, conflicts and transfer risk.
- Never turn correlation into causation.
- Never infer private identity, intent, endorsement, ownership, token control or official status.
- Separate cultural-product outcomes from financial outcomes.
- Treat price, volume, holders, followers, likes and impressions as adversarial or noisy metrics.
- Flag weak sources and contradictions.
- Choose the weakest sufficient hypothesis consistent with evidence.
- Do not recommend a DAO, wallet system, leaderboard, AI companion, database or social network without evidence that requires it.
- Do not edit websites, publish, send messages, interact with wallets, buy or sell anything, or modify product code.
