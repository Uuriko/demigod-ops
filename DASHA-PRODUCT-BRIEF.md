# Dasha product brief

Updated: 2026-08-06

## What the product is

Dasha has three deliberately small parts:

1. **Dasha Desk** — the `$dasha` landing page: a clear explanation, carefully sourced mint information, official links and product launcher.
2. **Dasha Thesis Card** — the first useful tool: write an asset thesis, confidence, deadline and invalidation condition, then make an X-ready card. The current local version does **not** prove when it was written.
3. **Dasha Discord** — the official home for community, support, memes, product feedback and future Rounds.

If real users repeatedly create and share Thesis Cards, the next product is a server-sealed public call with an unchanged original and automatic outcome resolution. That future layer would make “I said it before the move” credible. It is not built yet.

## What it is not

Dasha is not a trading terminal, token screener, wallet tracker, copy-trading product, signals room, safety oracle or P&L leaderboard. Existing products already serve those jobs, and adding them would increase security and promotion risk without strengthening the core idea.
