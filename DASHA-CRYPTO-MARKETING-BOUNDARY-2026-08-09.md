# Dasha crypto marketing boundary — 2026-08-09

Primary-source research on promotion, incentives, endorsements and territorial exposure. This is a
product risk map, not legal advice. It does not determine the operator's legal status, location,
audience targeting or available exemptions; those facts require qualified counsel before a regulated
promotion decision.

## Executive result

The durable product rule is **recognize participation, never purchase or promotional reach**.

Dasha can keep the quiz, Studio, Lobby, editorial founding row, creative/OSS recognition and a
zero-point holder badge because those mechanisms need not reward buying, referrals or favorable
public promotion. Do not add points, prizes, access, rank or status for:

- buying or holding `$dasha`;
- bag size, balance or transaction volume;
- likes, reposts, replies, views or follower count;
- referral conversions;
- posting a favorable claim about the token;
- leaving a positive review or suppressing a negative one.

If a future campaign gives anything of value for public content, the connection and conditions need
their own legal review and a clear disclosure attached to the content. The simpler default is not to
run that campaign.

## Current product evidence

- Home and Studio contain direct exact-mint `Buy $dasha` links to Jupiter.
- The site is globally reachable; no evidence reviewed here establishes UK exclusion or a lawful UK
  financial-promotion route.
- Simp Board explicitly gives zero points for likes, reposts, replies, referrals, purchases, token
  balances, bag size and payments.
- The holder badge scores zero and states only a dated positive raw-balance observation.
- Quiz points reward answer accuracy; sharing is optional and does not change the score.
- Creative and OSS points recognize reviewed work, not promotional sentiment or market activity.
- Perry's founding row is labeled editorial and non-measured.

The existing scoring boundary is better aligned with current marketing rules than an engagement or
holder leaderboard would be.

## United States

### SEC meme-coin statement is narrow and nonbinding

The SEC Division of Corporation Finance's February 2025 staff statement says the described meme
coins generally do not generate yield or rights to future income, profits or business assets, and
that transactions should be evaluated by their economic reality. It also says the statement is not
a rule, has no legal force, and does not prevent enforcement under other federal or state fraud laws.
A Commissioner published a contemporaneous dissent challenging the category-wide reasoning.

Dasha implication: do not treat the label “meme coin” or “culture project” as a safe harbor. Do not
promise yield, revenue share, profit participation, token utility or managerial efforts intended to
produce returns without a separate legal analysis.

Sources: [SEC staff statement](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/staff-statement-meme-coins),
[Commissioner Crenshaw response](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/crenshaw-response-staff-statement-meme-coins-022725).

### Incentivized social activity can be an endorsement

FTC guidance says a material connection can include payment, free products, discounts or other
perks; tags, likes and similar signals can be endorsements. Disclosures should be hard to miss and
travel with the endorsement rather than living only on a profile or about page. FTC staff also says
even an incentive without financial value can affect credibility. Its reviews rule prohibits
compensation conditioned on positive or negative review sentiment and prohibits knowingly buying
fake indicators of social influence.

Dasha implication: honor points or badges can still be a material connection when awarded for
promotional content. Do not award them for likes, reposts, referrals, reviews, purchase claims or
favorable token posts. If selected creative work is later reused as advertising and its creator
received value, preserve the relationship disclosure with that use.

Sources: [FTC influencer disclosures](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers),
[FTC endorsement questions](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking),
[FTC reviews rule Q&A](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/consumer-reviews-testimonials-rule-questions-answers).

## United Kingdom

The FCA says the cryptoasset financial-promotion regime applies to firms marketing qualifying
cryptoassets to UK consumers regardless of where the firm is based or which technology it uses.
Websites, apps and social media can be promotions. The FCA identifies four lawful communication
routes; communications outside those routes can breach section 21 FSMA. Rules for promotions made
through regulated routes can include prescribed warnings, fair/clear/non-misleading content,
appropriateness, client categorisation and a 24-hour cooling-off period. Incentives to invest and
refer-a-friend bonuses are restricted.

Dasha implication: a footer warning does not by itself create a lawful route. The unresolved question
is territorial/legal, not copywriting: determine with qualified counsel whether the current globally
reachable Buy CTAs are capable of having effect in the UK and, if so, whether an exemption, approval,
registration, or effective UK exclusion is required. Until that is resolved, do not add a wallet,
embedded swap, personalized offer, referral reward or other step that deepens the purchase journey.

Sources: [FCA marketing to UK consumers](https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/cryptoassets/marketing-uk-consumers),
[four lawful routes](https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/cryptoassets),
[current enforcement example](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/press-releases/fca-action-against-htx-illegal-financial-promotions),
[back-end requirements](https://www.fca.org.uk/publications/good-and-poor-practice/assessing-compliance-back-end-cryptoasset-financial-promotions-rules).

## European Union

MiCA Article 7 requires marketing communications relating to an offer to the public or admission to
trading of a crypto-asset other than an asset-referenced or e-money token to be identifiable as
marketing, fair, clear and not misleading. Where a white paper is required, the communication must
be consistent with it and cannot precede it; Article 7 also specifies a responsibility statement.
Transitional Article 143 applies Articles 7 and 9 to post-30 December 2024 marketing for covered
assets already admitted to trading.

Dasha implication: do not infer that an old token, a decentralized launch or the absence of custody
automatically removes marketing obligations. Determine the offeror/admission-to-trading facts and
territorial reach before adding EU-targeted acquisition campaigns or translating purchase copy for
EU distribution.

Sources: [MiCA Article 7](https://www.esma.europa.eu/publications-and-data/interactive-single-rulebook/mica/article-7-marketing-communications),
[MiCA Article 143](https://www.esma.europa.eu/publications-and-data/interactive-single-rulebook/mica/article-143-transitional-measures).

## Product controls

### Keep

- exact mint next to every acquisition route;
- external Jupiter handoff rather than embedded execution;
- zero purchase, holding, balance, referral and engagement points;
- zero-point holder badge with a dated, narrow basis;
- editorial labels and measured/non-measured separation;
- human review for creative and OSS recognition;
- no price targets, returns, yield, revenue share or safety claims;
- no automated posting, liking, reposting or review generation.

### Add to every future campaign review

1. What exact public behavior is requested?
2. Does the participant receive points, rank, access, money, tokens, discounts or another perk?
3. Could the content reasonably influence a purchase or perception of the project?
4. Is any sentiment, positive framing or token mention required explicitly or implicitly?
5. What jurisdictions can receive the communication?
6. What relationship disclosure travels with each reused post, image or testimonial?
7. Who reviews and retains the final copy, evidence and approval basis?

If questions 2 and 3 are both yes, treat it as an incentivized endorsement candidate. If question 4
is yes, reject the campaign by default.

## Ranked tasks

1. **Obtain a qualified territorial financial-promotion review** of the existing Home, Studio and
   how-to-buy acquisition links, especially UK and EU reach. This is the only task here that can
   resolve whether the current global presentation is legally supportable.
2. **Preserve the zero-point source list as a tested invariant.** Purchases, holdings, balances,
   payments, referrals and social engagement remain zero even if data becomes technically
   available. The public-copy gate rejects purchase-linked status phrased from either direction,
   including “holders get points,” “points for holding,” and “buy to earn rank.”
3. **Add a campaign-review gate before any raid, ambassador, creator-payment or points-for-posting
   experiment.** The gate records requested behavior, value, sentiment condition, audience and
   disclosure plan.
4. **Keep share actions user-initiated and score-neutral.** Quiz and Studio shares may carry results
   or artifacts; sharing itself never increases rank.
5. **Record operator/editorial relationships wherever their praise is reused as promotion.** An
   editorial Board row is already labeled; do not present it as independent measured popularity.
6. **Do not solve the UK question with disclaimer accumulation.** A required warning belongs inside a
   verified lawful route; otherwise it is compliance theater.

## Residual uncertainty

This research does not establish who legally offers the token, who sought admission to trading,
whether any territorial exemption applies, whether the site targets UK or EU consumers, or which
entity would make a promotion. It therefore cannot establish compliance. Re-check current primary
sources and obtain advice before changing acquisition geography, offering incentives, or launching
paid/token-related campaigns.
