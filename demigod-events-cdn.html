FAQ

Is this a job fair?

No. Small, human-curated intros. We decline bad matches to protect both sides.

Are the pipeline “events” real-time?

The state machine is real ops design. Public cards here are educational — live match data stays private until mutual yes.

What does 10% mean?

10% of first-year salary when a hire starts — typical contingency is 15–25%. No subscription. Stripe wiring still pending — confirmations by email from hello@.

Why mutual yes?

Firm-driven outreach alone can hire faster but raise quit risk when people never chose the role (Wharton / SMJ research). We only intro after both sides agree — and we start from the 90-day outcome.

How is this “talent engineering”?

a16z’s framing: talent engineers build systems so exceptional people find each other — with judgment, not spam. Demigod is the human matchmaking layer: systems support the intro; a human still earns every conversation.

How fast do you reply?

No SLA clock on this page or the live site. A human will follow up after review.
