## Product audit

### What works
- **One bet:** Meme Studio remix loop (line × look × format → PNG + `#fragment` state).
- **Honest conversion:** Jupiter + direct fallback + visible mint + risk copy.
- **Culture without fake official:** `@dash_eats` + public lines + desk stills.
- **Scrapped well:** Thesis/receipts named dead in brief — do not re-open.

### What’s broken as a *system*
1. **Ship lag is the product.** Disk Studio/home/desk outrun live; users experience the old system.
2. **Prepared prototypes pull attention** (relay, capsule, how-to-buy, logo lab) without nav discipline.
3. **Success is unmeasured.** Roadmap gates (20% export, 10% share, 5 creators) lack instrumentation.
4. **Identity tension:** marketing can slide coin-first; SIMPLIFY/Studio bet wants Studio-first.

## Competitive / market audit (web-aided)

- Market rewards **meme fluency + community** more than utility chrome.  
- Most meme launches die; differentiation is **loop people repeat**, not another chart embed.  
- Buy UX norms: **Jupiter / wallet**, mint check, rugcheck — Dasha already aligns.  
- Creative tools: **portable state** > empty canvas; Studio’s fragment URL is strategically correct.  
- Multiplayer collab products are heavy; Dasha should not fake “community software” early.
