# Horizon — things that could exist

**Nothing here is assigned, promised or scheduled.** This is the possibility space for the whole
project, written so anyone — inside or outside — can pick something up without asking.

Three roadmaps, three jobs, no overlap:

| Document | Answers |
|---|---|
| [`DASHA-ROADMAP.md`](DASHA-ROADMAP.md) | What we are doing now, and why |
| `dasha-desk/docs/ROADMAP.md` | What belongs in the Desk repository specifically |
| **This file** | What could exist at all |

Difficulty is a hint, not a gate: **S** an afternoon · **M** a weekend · **L** a real project ·
**XL** would change what Dasha is.

## Ambitious north-star possibility

**Open culture objects:** portable, editable media that render immediately, preserve a bounded source/parent relationship, state their reuse terms and remain usable without an account or original host. Dasha could become the first coherent living world made from them; the open renderer/format could later support other communities.

This is a possibility, not a platform commitment. The sequence is evidence-bound:

1. one person edits and exports one object;
2. another person opens its state and materially changes it;
3. a real lineage remains useful and consensual;
4. a second independent renderer passes shared fixtures;
5. only then consider discovery, federation, identity or economics.

The world stays porous: spectatorship is legitimate, newcomers do not need insider vocabulary, branches may disagree, and editorial continuity is never represented as approval by the real person. Do not use streaks, compulsory cadence, simulated Dasha replies or financial scarcity to manufacture ritual.

Reuse standards instead of inventing them: ordinary URLs/files for transport, explicit licenses for reuse, C2PA where durable media provenance is actually needed, and W3C social/annotation protocols only if cross-host interaction becomes real. A parent link is not proof of authorship, identity, permission or endorsement.

## Making things

- **New Studio looks.** The highest-value contribution anywhere in the project. A look is one
  function drawing one composition; add it and it inherits export, formats, GIF and remix links free. **S**
- **A pluggable look format**, so art arrives as a self-contained file and a pull request instead of
  a conversation. **M**
- **MP4/WebM export** for platforms that prefer video. **M**
- **Sound** — a two-second sting with the video. Slot reels, a coin drop, silence as a joke. **M**
- **Print export**: CMYK, bleed, 300dpi. Stickers are how a coin leaves the timeline. **M**
- **A one-page printable artifact** from a proven Transmission. Test keeping and physical sharing before NFTs or artificial rarity. **S**
- **Sticker packs** for Telegram and iMessage from one session. **M**
- **ASCII/plain-text looks** that go where images cannot — bios, commit messages, MOTDs. **S**
- **Comic strips and quote cards**, multi-panel. **M**
- **Direct manipulation** — drag the text, nudge the mark, choose the crop. **L**
- **Generative variants**: one line, sixteen compositions, keep the one that lands. **M**

## The character

- **More expressions and poses.** It has five; it wants twenty. **S**
- **A generative avatar** — cherry plus hats, glasses, grounds, deterministic from a seed, exported
  as a PFP. **L**
- **Animated loops**: blink, double-take, slow head turn. **M**
- **A second character**, if it is built from the same geometry. **M**

## Verification and trust

- **Deeper mint verification** — authorities, LP lock, holder concentration, each with the raw
  source linked so a reader can re-derive it. **M**
- **"What changed since you last looked"** — a local diff between visits, no account, no server. **M**
- **A rug-heuristics panel** that shows every check, its value and its limits, and refuses to emit a
  single safety score. The score is the lie; the checks are the product. **L**
- **A verification desk for any Solana mint.** The most useful thing this project could give the
  ecosystem, and the strongest proof the trust posture is real rather than marketing. **XL**
- **Explorer disagreement detection** — when independent sources disagree, say so loudly instead of
  quietly picking one. **M**
- **A scheduled public honesty audit** of the live site, published whether or not it flatters us. **M**

## Culture

- **A remix lineage graph.** Every Studio image already links its parent; draw the tree. Culture
  becomes a visible shape. **L**
- **A gallery of made things**, opt-in, never ranked by price or volume. **M**
- **Lore Vault** — submitted artifacts curated into a canon, each with provenance. **L**
- **A quote wall** that distinguishes what someone said from what a meme says they said. **M**
- **Seasonal kits** — looks that exist for two weeks then stop. Scarcity of form, not of supply. **M**
- **Creative bounties**, judged in public, paid in recognition. **M**

## Distribution

- **Embeddable widgets** others can drop into their own sites. Every embed is a doorway. **M**
- **A card-render service** so any shared link unfurls as the image it made. **L**
- **A CLI** — `npx dasha "your line"` writes a PNG. Developers are a channel nobody in this category
  is serving. **M**
- **A browser extension**: verify any mint on any page, make an image from any selection. **L**
- **A share-target PWA** so the Studio appears in the OS share sheet. **M**
- **On-chain actions** so a link can carry a verified buy. Highest consequence item here — it returns
  a transaction a stranger signs, so it needs a threat model, allowlisting, simulation and an
  incident plan before a line of it ships. **XL**

## Reusable by other projects

- **A design-token package** — palette, type scale, motifs, versioned. **S**
- **The kit as a library**: the mark, the character, the looks, importable and CC0. **M**
- **A headless renderer** for server-side images. **L**
- **A culture-coin starter** — all of this, genericised, so the next project begins with an honest
  desk and a working studio instead of a hero image and a promise. **XL**
- **A culture-object fixture set** — a tiny documented JSON recipe plus expected renders that a
  second implementation can open. No protocol before real cross-person remixing. **M**
- **A host-independent culture-object renderer** — open, deterministic and compatible with the
  fixture set; Dasha remains the flagship world rather than becoming generic templates. **XL**

## Always open

Never needs a proposal: accessibility passes, reduced-motion and high-contrast support,
performance, tests for anything currently checked by eye, documentation, and refactors that delete
more than they add.

## The few hard lines

Everything above is negotiable. These are not, and they are about not hurting people:

1. **No price predictions, targets, returns or urgency** — including jokes that stop being jokes once
   screenshotted.
2. **No fabricated traction**: invented holders, volume, endorsements or quotes.
3. **No implied endorsement by a real person**, and no real person's likeness used to promote the token.
4. **No custody of funds or keys**, and nothing mistakable for it.
5. **Third-party media needs recorded rights** before it ships.
6. **Security-sensitive findings go through the private path**, never a public issue.

Think a line is in the wrong place? Argue it in the open. Quietly shipping past it is the only
version that is not allowed.
