## In one minute

Dasha is a browser culture project next to Solana `$dasha`. Live system: **Home**, **Studio**, **Desk**, **Lobby**, optional X OAuth, opt-in **Simp Board**. Front door: [getdasha.com](https://www.getdasha.com/). Jobs: (1) identify and reach the associated mint via external swaps without custody; (2) make and share portable culture artifacts.
