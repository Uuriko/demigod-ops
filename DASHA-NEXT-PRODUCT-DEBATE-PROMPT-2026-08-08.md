# Dasha next-product debate brief

## Assignment

Determine what Dasha should work on or build next after the user rejected an over-elaborate ritual design. Do not assume that Transmission 001, the Meme Studio, or the current roadmap is correct merely because it is documented. Preserve only explicit hard boundaries. Produce a decision argument, not a feature dump.

State what you actually read. If context limits prevent exhaustive reading, name the omissions rather than claiming completeness.

## Current user signal

The proposed ritual with randomized constraints, verdicts, witnessing, continuity, and several mechanics felt convoluted. A reduced one-line alibi generator may be worth testing, but the user wants other ideas debated before committing. Dasha should feel elegant, concise, mysterious, fun, culturally distinctive, and visually strong. The website should make people want to join the community and engage with `$dasha`, while remaining honest about risk and association.

## Hard boundaries

- The Thesis Card, receipts, Pair, forecasting rounds, and descendants are permanently scrapped.
- No fake metrics, invented partnerships, price promises, holder surveillance, wallet custody, deceptive promotion, unofficial Telegram, or implied endorsement.
- Do not use complexity to rescue weak demand.
- Do not publish, post, message users, submit forms, move money, or modify project files.
- Distinguish the product from token conversion, the website from the product, and current evidence from aspiration.

## Debate questions

1. What is the simplest compelling reason for a person to return to Dasha even if they never buy the token?
2. Is a recurring creative prompt actually the best wedge, or is the evidence compatible with a different product category?
3. What can only Dasha plausibly do—not a generic meme generator, token landing page, Discord, or existing creator platform?
4. Which concepts create visible cultural output rather than dashboards, chores, or infrastructure?
5. Which idea is fun in under ten seconds, understandable without explanation, and capable of becoming deeper through repeated use?
6. What existing assets can test each idea without building a platform?
7. What would falsify each leading idea quickly?
8. What should explicitly not be built next?

## Required output

- Evidence observed, separated from interpretation.
- Five to eight materially different product directions. At least two must not depend on the Meme Studio or recurring prompts.
- For each: one-sentence experience, unique Dasha advantage, smallest test, success signal, kill signal, and principal trust/operational risk.
- Rank the top three using delight, distinctiveness, ten-second clarity, distribution, reuse of existing assets, reversibility, and evidence quality.
- Argue strongly against your own top choice.
- Name one final recommendation and a runner-up.
- Specify the smallest concrete artifact to make next, capped at one surface and one primary action.
- List the decisive unresolved disagreement another agent should challenge.

Avoid accounts, feeds, points, leaderboards, AI chatbots, token gating, marketplaces, and elaborate lore unless evidence makes one indispensable. Prefer a sharp cultural object or behavior over a feature system.
