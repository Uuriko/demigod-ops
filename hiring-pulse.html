The finding

YC startups barely hire until a year after their batch

5.1×more likely to be hiring a year+ past their batch

Just 9% of the newest cohort (Summer 2026) is hiring; 46% of companies a year or more past their batch are. If you're job-hunting, the fresh batch isn't where the openings are.

Method. Open-role counts are live US-posted or Remote listings on each company’s own public Greenhouse/Lever/Ashby/Workable job board (plus owner-gated secondary ATS when joined).
