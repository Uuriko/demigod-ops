## C. Findings for all 30 tracks

### 1. Why people return to small creative communities

**Finding:** People join and stay in *small* communities for hyperspecificity, legible norms, and group identity—not because size maximizes information or dyadic friendship.

- **Hwang & Foote (2021), CSCW / arXiv:2108.04282** — **Q**, n=20 Reddit interviews. Small communities provide unique informational niches; participation is a *curation strategy* over a large ecosystem; identity is group-based more than friendship-based. Tier A for mechanism; transfer risk: Reddit hobby subs ≠ culture coin.  
- **Joyce & Kraut (2006)** — **O**, 2,777 newcomers, six newsgroups: *any reply* → return 44%→56%; positivity/length not independent predictors. Tier A for first-response design.  
- **DS106 Daily Create** — **P/OP**: ~15–20 min prompts, no registration, no prizes, continuous since 2012 (5,000+ daily prompts claimed). Persistence without rewards is possible when the ritual is tiny.

**WSH:** Return is produced by (clear role + witnessed contribution + niche identity), not by growth dashboards.

### 3. Memecoin communities that survived or collapsed after attention/price shocks

**Finding:** Mass launch platforms produce near-total cultural death; survivors tend to keep a **legible identity + distribution rails**, not a feature checklist. Survival of *price* ≠ survival of *culture*.

| Case | Pattern | Evidence type | Transfer |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pump.fun cohort** | <2% graduate to major DEXes; Q4 2024 analysis; millions of mints | **O** / **PP** | Strong for base rates of death |
| **Manipulation industrial complex** | Snipers, wash volume, comment bots manufacture activity | **O** / **PP** | Price/volume unusable as culture metric |
| **PEPE / WIF / BONK** | Identity + organic meme mutation (WIF chaos; BONK distribution story) | **C** market narrative | Causal claims about “why they lived” are weak |
| **Political/hype spikes (2025 recaps)** | Fast attention, fast collapse | **C** | Attention is not retention |

Practitioner narratives claim PEPE/WIF/BONK “communities” endure via insider language and identity; these are **not** controlled studies and mix financial and cultural survival.

**WSH:** After a shock, communities that still *make and recognize shared jokes* can continue as culture even if price dies; communities that only traded signals disappear when the chart dies. Dasha has not yet proven the former.

### 4. Branching remix lineages and collective creativity

**Finding:** Systems that force each work to *start from a previous work* produce deep chains and structured play—not only parallel posts.

- **Tuite & Smith (2012), AIIDE** — **O**, >50k sketches in anonymous collaborative art: deep chains, conversation, structured play. Supports “change one thing, pass it on.”  
- **Scratch remix research** — **Q/O**: attribution is social; technical permission ≠ perceived originality.  
- **Thingiverse remix graphs** — **O**: knowledge heterogeneity and remix co-vary; platform-specific.  
- **Magma / ForkArt** — **OP**: product category exists for visible lineage *inside* a host.  

**WSH for Dasha:** Remix Relay is the correct *mechanism* test. Hosted lineage UIs are optional after chains exist.

### 5. Recognition that preserves novelty

**Finding:** Recognition increases output; single total ranks and peer awards can *reduce novelty* and intensify harmful comparison.

| Mechanism | Effect on volume | Effect on novelty | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Any first reply | ↑ return | n/a | Joyce & Kraut **O** |
| Peer awards (Reddit) | ↑ posts especially for newcomers | ↓ novelty / more similarity to awarded work | Burtch et al. **E** |
| Absolute leaderboards | Mixed / can hurt low ranks | comparison pressure | experimental **E** |
| Multi-category editorial showcase | unproven at Dasha scale | preserves diversity if categories rotate | design inference |

**Design implication already in Dasha research:** Accomplices / Mechanics / Archivists / Witnesses before one score. Never bag-size rank.

### 8. Motivations for sharing creative artifacts

- Entertainment value for others  
- Belonging / in-group signaling  
- Status / recognition  
- Altruism / usefulness

Meme-specific work lists virality, humor, authenticity, cultural relevance as distinct motivations—not “more formats.” Transfer: **C/O**.

**For Dasha:** Sharing is more likely when the artifact *signals belonging to a joke* and when the next person can continue it—not when the UI offers more aspect ratios alone.

### 9. Cross-platform artifact propagation and provenance survival

**Finding:** Flattened images travel; signed/embedded provenance often dies on social CDNs.

- C2PA / Content Credentials: open standard for signed history (**OP**).  
- Multiple 2025–2026 analyses: Instagram, X, TikTok pipelines commonly strip manifests on recompress (**O/P**).  
- Screenshots destroy provenance.  
- Dasha’s fragment recipe is a *reconstruction* carrier, not a C2PA replacement—and that is a strength for editability.

**Answer to explicit question:** *Can a meme remain editable and attributable after leaving its original platform?*  
**Yes for editability if** the recipe travels *beside* the image (URL fragment, companion link, OCO).  
**Attribution** survives socially (credit norms) better than cryptographically unless platforms preserve credentials.  
**Do not rely on PNG metadata alone.**

### 10. Human–AI collective creativity and responsible creation

**Evidence pattern:** AI can raise individual novelty for weaker writers while increasing homogenization at group level (multiple 2024–2026 studies; mixed designs). AI also floods OSS maintainers with low-quality PRs.

**Responsible creation for Dasha:**

- Prefer human-authored lines on project starters.  
- Label synthetic likeness / voice if ever used (legal + ethical).  
- Reject autonomous “Dasha bot” posting as culture (endorsement + metric poisoning).  
- Studio procedural looks are already non-photo generative—lower likeness risk than face models.

### 11. Human-readable provenance

**Finding:** Interfaces that make source *part of the social experience* outperform hidden metadata. Creative Commons platform toolkit: Title, Author, Source, License at download/remix; attribution ≠ endorsement.

**Dasha-ready stack (already aligned):** transmission id · display name · parent link · license state · non-endorsement line · material-change flag.

**Entertaining vs administrative:** ForkArt/Magma treat lineage as product story. Scratch shows credit fights when similarity is high. **Test:** one-hop “From” as a cultural object (“this is the child of X”), not a paperwork form.

## H. Manipulation-resistant measurement framework

### Principle

If a metric can be bought cheaper than genuine participation, it is not a success metric.

### Primary metrics (count, don’t composite)

1. Distinct non-operator creators (human judgment + multi-signal)  
2. Materially changed artifacts (look/format/line vs parent)  
3. Exports and voluntary share/submit intents  
4. First-submission acknowledgement rate + median time  
5. Return within season (2nd act)  
6. Cross-person remixes  
7. Source/permission field completeness  
8. Contribution-type diversity (image/line/code/docs/moderation)  
9. Concentration (share of top 1 / top 3 creators)  
10. Consent/takedown incidents  

### Explicitly excluded from pass/fail

Price, mcap, volume, holders, followers, likes, RTs, impressions, raid counts, bot comments, wallet size.

### Anti-gaming controls

- Season caps  
- No points for reactions  
- Operator examples tagged and excluded  
- Manual review for first experiments  
- Publish methods, not a single “health score”  
- Treat sudden spikes as *suspicious until proven*

### Optional later (only with abuse model)

Opt-in holder badge (flat); OSS impact points (already designed with caps).

## L. Ten cheapest field studies (research designs only)

1. **First-ack A/B (manual):** Randomize half of first submitters to same-day human reply vs 72h delay; measure 7-day second act. **E**, n≥40 if possible.  
2. **PNG vs remix-link matched relay:** Same 10 seeds, two arms; count material 2nd gen. (Relay Lab already prepared.)  
3. **Starter completeness 3-arm:** blank / incomplete line / polished finished; measure material-change rate and uniqueness (pairwise text/image distance).  
4. **Polished wall vs honest wall:** gallery of 8 flawless operator pieces vs 8 varied imperfect ones; measure subsequent originality (observational or sequential).  
5. **Acknowledgement-only showcase:** feature 5 works with no points for 14 days; measure repeat rate vs prior baseline.  
6. **Coin-removed creative session:** invite non-crypto creative friends; no mint UI; measure completion & delight interviews (**Q**).  
7. **Provenance entertainment test:** show lineage as “family tree joke” vs metadata table; preference + share intent.  
8. **Transmission pull test:** run ep1; ship ep2 only if pull criteria met; pre-register pass/fail.  
9. **Farming probe:** announce points-for-upload for 48h in a closed test; measure low-effort flood (then kill). Ethical: disclose test.  
10. **Consent comprehension:** 5-minute study—can participants correctly restate reuse terms after submit UI? Fail if <80% correct on critical items.
