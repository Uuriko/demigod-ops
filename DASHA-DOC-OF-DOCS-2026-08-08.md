## Suggested doc hygiene (from multi-agent debate)

1. Only **six product/operations owners** stay “Current” in the map: Docs, Workflow, Simplify, Bible, Brief, Roadmap (+ Domain/Webflow when shipping). Dated research informs owners; it does not become another strategy owner.  
2. Mark OPEN-CULTURE-OBJECTS and DISCORD as **Experiment / gated**, not “Active product specification.”  
3. Move one-day LOOP-PROMPT piles and Demigod mixed docs out of mental “Dasha current.”  
4. Prefer **deleting or archiving** over writing a 25th strategy doc.

The complete improvement backlog now lives in [`DASHA-DOCS-SYSTEM-BACKLOG.md`](DASHA-DOCS-SYSTEM-BACKLOG.md); do not grow a second hygiene list here.

## External research anchors (2026-08-08 pack)

- Memecoins are culture + attention, not utility; high failure rate (~97% first-year claims in public writeups).  
- Survivors: liquidity + community engagement through drawdowns, not whitepapers.  
- Solana path: Jupiter / wallet browser; verify mint; distrust DM links (Trust/Solflare guidance).  
- Creative tools win on **portable editable state**, not empty editors or accounts first.
