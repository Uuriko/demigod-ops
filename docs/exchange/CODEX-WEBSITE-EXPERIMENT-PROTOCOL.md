# Codex website experiment protocol

**Date:** 2026-07-16  
**Product:** Demigod — SF startup talent matching, tech-assisted and human-reviewed  
**Phase:** Pre-services honesty; no invented inventory, proof, speed, or operational capability  
**Purpose:** Generate a real alternative to **Operator Calm**, choose it on evidence, then implement one homepage once.

## 1. Non-negotiable product brief

Every concept must communicate these facts in the first viewport:

1. Demigod matches people with SF startups.
2. There are two first-class paths: **I'm hiring** and **I'm looking**.
3. Software structures and ranks fit; a person reviews potential matches.
4. An introduction occurs only after both sides express interest.

The employer path may lead the hierarchy, but talent may not look like secondary inventory. The site may say **10% of first-year cash compensation, only when someone starts**, **free for talent**, and **billing setup is pending**. It may not imply live roles, a candidate pool, customers, placement volume, a response time, an SLA, a guaranteed shortlist, automatic hiring, replacement terms, live billing, or named proof that does not exist.

All concepts use the existing branched WIZ. They differ in information architecture and visual argument—not in product truth.

## 2. Six homepage experiments

### A. The Match Table

**Thesis:** Make the homepage feel like a product interface, not a recruiter brochure.

**IA:** Compact nav → split decision header → full-width abstract match table → three-gate explanation → single pricing strip → footer.

**First viewport:** Left third carries the proposition and two equal path buttons. Right two-thirds is a large, fictional-data-free table with columns `Signal`, `Tech rank`, `Human review`, `Mutual interest`. Three abstract rows move through states; no names, scores, counts, or timestamps.

**Visual system:** Off-white canvas; near-black table; electric cyan status accent; condensed labels with a system grotesk body; hard 1px grid; 4px corners; no shadows. It should resemble an operator console softened for public use. Motion is one state transition on load, then still.

**Copy outline:**

- Eyebrow: `SF STARTUP TALENT MATCHING`
- H1: `Fit, with receipts.`
- Subhead: `Demigod tech ranks the evidence. A human reviews the tradeoffs. Both sides approve before an intro.`
- Paths: `I'm hiring — Share a role` / `I'm looking — Share my profile`
- Mechanism: `Signal in` → `Ranked` → `Reviewed` → `Both interested`
- Price: `10% on hire. Free for talent. Billing setup is pending.`

**Risk to test:** May feel too much like a mature SaaS product. The abstract artifact must be labeled `HOW THE PROCESS WORKS`, never presented as a live dashboard.

### B. Two Doors, One Standard

**Thesis:** Turn the dual audience from a navigation problem into the identity of the page.

**IA:** Wordmark only → two 50/50 full-height doors → shared center seam explaining the mechanism → audience-specific detail revealed below → price → footer.

**First viewport:** The screen is split vertically. Hiring is ink on pale coral; talent is cream on deep aubergine. Each half has one sentence and one large action. A narrow center rail reads `TECH RANKS → HUMAN REVIEWS → BOTH APPROVE` and visually joins the halves.

**Visual system:** Editorial poster; very large type; asymmetric color pair with identical spatial weight; square edges; no cards; oversized arrow glyphs; almost no motion beyond a 2% expansion on hover/focus. On mobile the halves stack 50/50 above the fold, hiring first but with equal height and type scale.

**Copy outline:**

- Shared kicker: `ONE MATCH. TWO YESes.`
- Hiring H1: `Build the team.`
- Hiring support: `Share the role and the 90-day outcome.`
- Talent H1: `Find the fit.`
- Talent support: `Share your work, skills, and constraints.`
- Shared seam: `Demigod structures fit. A human reviews it. Identity stays private until mutual interest.`
- Terms: `10% when a hire starts · free for talent`

**Risk to test:** Graphic drama could outrun trust. The mechanism and terms must remain readable without hover and at 390px.

### C. The Brief

**Thesis:** The strongest product proof is the quality of the intake question, especially the 90-day outcome.

**IA:** Minimal nav → giant “brief” card with one audience toggle → sample blank fields (not sample answers) → mutual-consent diagram → terms → footer.

**First viewport:** A paper-like form dominates. The first visible prompt is `What should be true 90 days after this person starts?` Hiring and talent tabs change the field labels but never submit from the mock artifact. Beside it, concise copy and the real WIZ CTAs explain why better inputs create better matches.

**Visual system:** Warm white, black ink, tomato-red annotation marks, monospaced field labels, serif display headline using an already available/system serif fallback. Ruled lines and margin notes replace conventional cards. No fake filled-in answers.

**Copy outline:**

- H1: `Start with what success means.`
- Subhead: `A title is not enough. Demigod structures the outcome, evidence, and constraints before ranking fit.`
- Artifact labels: `90-day outcome`, `Evidence`, `Constraints`, `What would make this a no?`
- Human layer: `Tech narrows the field. A person reviews context and tradeoffs.`
- Consent: `No identity handoff until both sides are interested.`
- Paths: `Brief a role` / `Brief my profile`

**Risk to test:** Could look like consulting or a form company. The SF startup talent category must be explicit in the top 100 vertical pixels.

### D. Mutual Yes

**Thesis:** Lead with the marketplace's trust mechanism rather than matching technology.

**IA:** Nav → central mutual-approval interaction → two audience explanations → rank/review process → one pricing block → compact FAQ link → footer.

**First viewport:** Two large circles labeled `Hiring team` and `Talent` overlap only after two visible `Interested` states. Before overlap, a lock label says `Private`. The real CTAs sit under their respective circles. The diagram uses no profiles, avatars, or implied live activity.

**Visual system:** Pale blue canvas; navy text; acid-lime approval accent used sparingly; circular geometry; generous whitespace; friendly but not consumer-cute. Motion may demonstrate `private → mutual → intro` once and must expose the same sequence as text.

**Copy outline:**

- H1: `An intro takes two yeses.`
- Subhead: `Demigod tech surfaces fit. A human reviews the match. Each side decides privately.`
- Hiring: `See relevant evidence before identity moves.`
- Talent: `Review the opportunity before contact is shared.`
- Mechanism: `Ranked by fit` → `Human reviewed` → `Mutual interest` → `Intro`
- Terms: `10% only when someone starts · always free for talent`

**Risk to test:** Consent can explain the last mile while obscuring sourcing and fit. The ranking and review sentence must be prominent, not buried below the fold.

### E. SF Signal Map

**Thesis:** Own geographic and startup specificity without pretending to own a network.

**IA:** Place-led hero → dual paths → abstract Bay Area signal map → “what enters a match” → three gates → terms → footer.

**First viewport:** Large typographic `SF` anchors the composition. Fine lines connect unlabeled, non-geographic nodes representing `Outcome`, `Work`, `Skills`, and `Constraints`, converging on `Reviewed fit`. This is a conceptual signal map, explicitly not a map of members or openings.

**Visual system:** Fog gray, international orange, dark evergreen; transit-diagram linework; wide grotesk type; utilitarian legends; no skyline photography, founder clichés, neighborhood pins, or invented density. Motion draws the four input lines once.

**Copy outline:**

- Eyebrow: `BUILT FOR SF STARTUP TEAMS + TALENT`
- H1: `More signal. Less startup noise.`
- Subhead: `Demigod structures what the role needs and what the person brings, then combines tech ranking with human review.`
- Paths: `I'm hiring` / `I'm looking`
- Inputs: `90-day outcome`, `Evidence of work`, `Skills`, `Constraints`
- Rule: `No intro without mutual interest.`

**Risk to test:** Local specificity can sound like existing marketplace liquidity. Never use `network`, member dots, “in SF now,” supply counts, or proximity claims.

### F. The Anti-Job-Board

**Thesis:** Position by subtraction: private, deliberate matching instead of public listings and application volume.

**IA:** Contrarian headline → “not this / this” comparison → dual path actions → process → price → footer.

**First viewport:** A stark diagonal or horizontal comparison. One side is crossed-out noise—`Public listings`, `Application piles`, `Cold intros`—as category conventions, not claims about competitors. The other side is `Outcome`, `Evidence`, `Review`, `Mutual interest`, ending in the two WIZ paths.

**Visual system:** Newsprint cream, black, safety red; bold sans; visible strike rules; compact editorial columns; deliberately low decoration. No competitor logos and no aggressive “AI replaces recruiters” framing.

**Copy outline:**

- H1: `Not another pile of applications.`
- Subhead: `Demigod matches SF startup roles and talent around evidence, constraints, and the outcome that matters.`
- Contrast: `Volume → Signal` / `Public → Private` / `One-sided outreach → Mutual interest`
- Mechanism: `Tech-assisted ranking. Human review before intro.`
- Paths: `Share a role` / `Share my profile`
- Terms: `10% when someone starts. Free for talent.`

**Risk to test:** Negativity can consume the brand. The positive method must occupy at least half the first viewport and all comparison claims must remain defensible.

## 3. Scoring rubric

Score each concept from **0–5** on every row, multiply by weight, and divide the total by five for a score out of 100. A 3 means competent, a 4 means strong, and a 5 means unusually clear or distinctive. Score from rendered desktop and mobile frames, not prose descriptions.

| Criterion | Weight | What earns 5 |
|---|---:|---|
| Category clarity | 15 | A stranger identifies SF startup talent matching in three seconds. |
| Audience parity | 15 | Hiring and talent paths are equally obvious, credible, and actionable on both breakpoints. |
| Mechanism clarity | 12 | Tech ranking, human review, and mutual approval are understood without scrolling or animation. |
| Conversion focus | 12 | Each audience has one unmistakable action that opens the correct WIZ branch. |
| Pre-services honesty | 12 | No implied liquidity, customers, speed, automation, operations, or proof; terms are precise. |
| Trust and privacy | 8 | Private review and mutual interest are concrete without agency theater. |
| Distinctiveness | 8 | The blurred screenshot is memorable and materially unlike the current dark/gold page and generic HR SaaS. |
| Mobile integrity | 7 | The concept survives 390×844 with parity, readable hierarchy, 48px targets, and no dependence on hover. |
| Accessibility | 6 | Semantic order, contrast, focus, reduced motion, and non-color equivalents are designed in. |
| Implementation fitness | 5 | Permanent DOM fits Designer; behavior reuses WIZ; no new dependency or foot-built homepage. |

### Hard gates

A concept is disqualified regardless of total score if any reviewer finds:

- a fabricated or ambiguous proof signal;
- a missing or subordinate talent path;
- no explicit human-review step;
- an intro shown without mutual interest;
- unreadable or misleading mobile behavior;
- a homepage that requires foot-core to construct its stable DOM;
- a first viewport that cannot state the category without animation.

Advance at most two concepts to high-fidelity frames. Choose one only if it scores **80+**, has no hard-gate failure, and beats Operator Calm by at least **5 points** or wins a clearly documented strategic tradeoff. Otherwise Operator Calm remains the baseline.

## 4. Debate agenda: challenger versus Operator Calm

The debate compares the highest-scoring challenger with Operator Calm. It is a 45-minute evidence review, not a taste vote.

1. **Silent read, 3 minutes:** Show 1440×900 and 390×844 first viewports without concept names. Each reviewer writes the product, audiences, mechanism, and expected next action.
2. **Comprehension reveal, 7 minutes:** Compare those answers. Count misunderstandings; do not explain the designs yet.
3. **Challenger case, 6 minutes:** State what strategic idea the challenger owns that Operator Calm does not. Name the single most important conversion hypothesis.
4. **Operator Calm case, 6 minutes:** Defend clarity, trust, implementation restraint, and why visual calm is appropriate before proof exists.
5. **Steelman swap, 6 minutes:** Each side names the other direction's strongest advantage and its own most dangerous failure mode.
6. **Dual-audience stress, 5 minutes:** Trace one hiring visitor and one talent visitor from landing through WIZ open. Flag any hierarchy, privacy, or language asymmetry.
7. **Honesty prosecution, 5 minutes:** Treat every artifact, diagram, status, and term as a claim. Remove or relabel anything that could imply live supply or operating maturity.
8. **Build-cost reality, 4 minutes:** Identify permanent Designer DOM, head-critical CSS, and foot behavior. Reject any direction whose distinction depends on runtime reconstruction.
9. **Score and decide, 3 minutes:** Apply the rubric independently, record totals and hard gates, then choose: `Challenger`, `Operator Calm`, or `Neither—repair one named defect and rescore`.

The debate may merge copy or accessibility fixes into the winner. It may not produce a seventh hybrid visual direction.

## 5. Webflow experiment workflow without thrash

### Ownership by layer

| Surface | Use it for | Do not use it for |
|---|---|---|
| Webflow MCP Data | Site/page metadata, SEO, assets, CMS, Analyze, publish, and agent instructions. | Canvas layout or large Custom Code payloads. |
| Webflow MCP Designer + Bridge | Permanent semantic homepage DOM, classes, visual styles, breakpoint layout, reusable static components, and stable `data-dg-*` hooks. | Repeated live experiments or product-runtime logic. |
| Head CSS | Tokens and critical above-fold stability, focus defaults, reduced motion, and narrowly bounded FOUC protection. | A second full theme or content repair layer. |
| Foot CDN (`demigod-foot-core.js`) | Existing WIZ branches, validation, submission, modal focus, consent behavior, routing, and minimal progressive enhancement. | Hero/nav/footer/process/pricing construction, copy sniffing, or experiment-specific megastyles. |
| Footer loader | One immutable CDN pin. | Markup, copy, or behavior. |

### Experiment sequence

1. Run `bin/dg-webflow connect`; open the Bridge with `bin/dg-webflow connect bridge`; confirm design mode, homepage, and breakpoint before touching the canvas.
2. Record the current layer/version truth and capture baseline frames. Check current coordination claims and the foot lock; do not race another writer.
3. Build all six concepts as low-fidelity static frames first. These may be local sketches or unpublished Designer sections, but never six live versions and never six foot branches.
4. Score the low-fidelity frames. Keep at most two. Delete rejected experiment DOM rather than hiding it indefinitely.
5. Build the finalists in one unpublished Designer pass using shared primitives only where they already exist. Use stable hooks such as `data-dg-path="hire"` and `data-dg-path="talent"`; the exact visual classes stay in Designer.
6. Wire finalists to the existing WIZ only after their static hierarchy passes. If a hook adapter is necessary, make one locked foot edit after the winner is selected—not one edit per concept.
7. Debate, select one winner, and remove the losing DOM/classes before publish. There is one canonical homepage, not an A/B framework.
8. Put only critical tokens/layout in head CSS. Keep the static page honest and usable with foot JS blocked.
9. Use the existing `bin/dg` ship spine for the coordinated Designer + head + pinned-foot release: lock, CDN pin if changed, CM6 paste for large Custom Code, publish, then hash/live truth. Do not invent a parallel paste or publish script.
10. Publish once after the full test matrix passes. Version equality proves deployment, while screenshots and task tests prove the redesign.

### Anti-thrash rules

- One homepage owner, one active Designer session, one foot writer lock.
- No live “color tests,” incremental polish ships, or version bumps as design evidence.
- No more inspiration research during this experiment; the supplied reference set is sufficient.
- No new library, A/B platform, animation framework, or design-system project.
- Prefer native HTML/CSS and Webflow interactions; motion must explain a product state.
- Static false claims are removed in Designer. Runtime scrubbers remain last-resort trust-boundary protection, not content management.
- Preserve the WIZ and its review-before-submit behavior. Redesign its shell only if the winning concept exposes a concrete usability defect.

## 6. Test plan

### Required capture matrix

Capture the baseline and winner with identical content and scroll positions:

| Mode | Viewport | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Desktop | 1440×900 | First viewport, full page, WIZ hiring open, WIZ talent open |
| Mobile | 390×844 | First viewport, full page, sticky action state if present, both WIZ branches |
| Narrow mobile | 320×568 | First viewport and longest WIZ field state; no clipping or horizontal scroll |
| JS blocked | 1440×900 and 390×844 | Honest static page, visible dual paths or truthful fallback, no stale canvas flash |
| Reduced motion | 1440×900 and 390×844 | Complete meaning without sequencing or transforms |

Also blur the two first-viewport screenshots. The winner must remain visibly different from the current site and preserve two clear action masses when copy cannot be read.

### Three-second comprehension

For each desktop and mobile first viewport, a reviewer answers without scrolling:

- What is this?
- Who is it for?
- What does technology do?
- What does a person do?
- When does an intro happen?
- What would I click as hiring / talent?

Pass: category and both paths are correct in at least 4 of 5 cold reads; no reader describes a job board, staffing agency, autonomous recruiter, or existing network.

### WIZ task tests

Run the existing hiring and talent branches from every visible CTA, nav action, and mobile sticky action.

**Hiring:** Open → correct audience state → role/company fields → required 90-day outcome → review screen → consent/submit behavior.  
**Talent:** Open → correct audience state → profile/work/skills/constraints → review screen → consent/submit behavior.

Pass criteria:

- one click opens the correct branch;
- back/next preserves entered values;
- required fields block progression with a useful, focused error;
- the review step appears before submit;
- modal focus is trapped and restored; Escape/close behaves correctly;
- success and failure states do not claim a response time or operational capability;
- `hello@trydemigod.com will follow up` is used where contact follow-up is promised;
- keyboard-only and screen-reader order follow the visible sequence;
- 48px minimum targets, no body scroll leak, no mobile keyboard obstruction.

### Honesty prosecution

Inspect visible text, accessibility names, metadata, structured data, hidden responsive variants, JS-off DOM, WIZ success/error states, and product artifacts for:

- fake logos, people, roles, counts, scores, timestamps, activity, or testimonials;
- `48h`, fast/instant language, SLA, shortlist quantity, placement guarantee, replacement promise;
- live Stripe/Twilio/SMS/payment implications instead of `pending` where relevant;
- “hand matched” or fully autonomous language;
- public identity sharing before mutual interest;
- `90-day outcome` presented as a guaranteed result rather than an intake criterion;
- more than three clearly labeled seed roles or any seed presented as real;
- founder names or the wrong contact language.

Any ambiguous artifact is treated as a claim and must be relabeled or removed.

### Visual, responsive, and accessibility checks

- No overflow at 320, 390, 768, 1024, and 1440 widths.
- Heading hierarchy and DOM order remain logical when CSS is removed.
- Body text meets 4.5:1 contrast; focus is visible on every interactive element.
- Hover-only information has a persistent or focus equivalent.
- Animation stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`; no perpetual motion, parallax, or scroll hijack.
- The two audience actions remain equal in prominence on mobile.
- The first content does not jump after foot load; no hidden legacy section flashes.
- Pricing reads `10%` with its basis and pre-services qualification in the same visual block.

### Existing gates and release proof

Before publish, run the existing source verification plus targeted board-honesty and loop-state checks. After publish, verify live body/version hash through the canonical truth path, inspect console errors, repeat the desktop/mobile WIZ smoke, and recapture the same screenshots.

Acceptance requires all of the following:

1. Rubric score at least 80 with no hard-gate failure.
2. Both WIZ paths complete at desktop and mobile.
3. Honesty prosecution finds no unresolved claim.
4. Source, board-honesty, loop-state, and live truth gates pass.
5. Blurred before/after screenshots show a different hierarchy and visual system.
6. Stable homepage content lives in Designer, while foot-core remains product behavior rather than page construction.
