## Positioning one-liner

> **Demigod gives SF seed startups human-curated, mutual-yes talent intros built around the hire's 90-day outcome—for 10% on hire: less noise than Wellfound, open beyond YC's Work at a Startup, and leaner than a 15–25% agency.**

This is the strongest current wedge because it names the audience, method, outcome frame, price, and three alternatives without pretending Demigod has marketplace scale or placement proof it does not yet have.

## Features not to build pre-PMF

| Do not build yet | Why it is premature | Trigger to reconsider |
|------------------|---------------------|-----------------------|
| **AI recruiter / matching autopilot** | Automates a process whose winning judgment and data are not yet known; weakens the human-curated wedge. | Repeated briefs and enough reviewed match outcomes to identify stable patterns. |
| **ATS or recruiting CRM suite** | Competes on workflow breadth with incumbents while demand is the actual bottleneck. | Paying founders repeatedly request shared pipeline management. |
| **Public national job board or candidate marketplace** | Creates cold-start noise, moderation work, and a direct fight with Wellfound/LinkedIn. | Dense, repeatable supply and demand in the initial SF seed niche. |
| **Founder self-serve candidate browsing** | Encourages shopping and bypasses mutual consent before trust and supply quality are proven. | Founders have enough approved candidates to browse without harming privacy or match quality. |
| **OAuth / LinkedIn profile import polish** | Saves little time at current intake volume and does not create demand. | At least 10 wizard starts per week or measured form abandonment attributable to profile entry. |
| **Live Twilio/SMS automation** | Adds operational and compliance surface before a manual communication loop is validated. | Real pilots show SMS is the preferred channel and manual follow-up is a bottleneck. |
| **Automated Stripe checkout, billing portal, or subscription plans** | The model is success-fee based and can be invoiced manually during pilots; checkout does not win the first brief. | A real hire requires collection or repeat volume makes manual invoicing unreliable. |
| **Recommendation engine, scoring system, or “matching OS”** | Premature precision can conceal weak evidence and encode assumptions from sample data. | A meaningful set of completed, human-reviewed intros and outcomes exists. |
| **Role/location SEO page factory** | Produces surface area before the service has proof, focus, or inventory. | A repeatable niche converts and search demand supports a small number of real pages. |
| **Logo wall, counters, synthetic testimonials, or fake board growth** | These are not features but false proof; they create durable trust risk. | Never synthesize. Add only permissioned, verifiable customer or pilot evidence. |
| **Webflow rewrite or new app stack** | The current site is sufficient for GTM; migration creates risk without addressing demand starvation. | A measured product limitation blocks repeated qualified conversion after PMF evidence. |

## Bottom line

Demigod should sell a deliberately narrow service before building a broad platform: secure one founder brief, manually produce a few excellent mutual-yes intros, and turn that real work into proof. Until that loop repeats, sharper outreach and trustworthy LP framing are higher leverage than additional software.
