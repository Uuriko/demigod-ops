## Theme map (Demigod mechanisms)

### T1. Two-sided matching & market design
**Evidence:** Roth/Peranson NRMP; Army DA RCT; Eeckhout sorting survey; entry-market unraveling/congestion.  
**For Demigod:** Mutual structure beats one-sided spam; Demigod is *thin-N human-mediated*, not a national DA engine—use Roth for *why mutual consent matters*, not for building an algorithm match day.  
**Brainstorm prompts:**
- What would “stable enough” mean at 1–5 intros/month?
- Which congestion problems (apply volume, early exploding offers) does mutual-yes already kill?

### T2. Mutual yes / double opt-in intros
**Evidence:** Fred Wilson double opt-in intro; investor/founder etiquette blogs; daily.dev Recruiter marketing; Underdog “anonymous until mutual match.”  
**For Demigod:** Strong cultural + product precedent for **both sides approve before identity moves**. Distinct from email marketing double opt-in, same behavioral root (agency, anti-spam).  
**Brainstorm prompts:**
- How explicit is the second “yes” in copy and UX today?
- Should talent always see base band *before* company sees identity (Hired/Underdog-like)?

### T3. Software compares → human proposes
**Evidence:** Horton JOLE (recommendations raise fills); algorithmic bias surveys; Cowgill humans-vs-machines; Ashby AI review as *tools* not matchers.  
**For Demigod:** Academic support for **recommendation quality moving hires**; academic warning against **auto-endorsement / fairness theater**. Fits “software compares, human proposes.”  
**Brainstorm prompts:**
- What is the minimum evidence set for a “compare” to be non-theater?
- Where does human override get logged for honesty?

### T4. Reverse-apply & cash transparency
**Evidence:** Roussille/Scuderi Hired.com; Wellfound salary/equity defaults; Underdog privacy.  
**For Demigod:** Putting **role + base band** in front of talent before intro is research-aligned and competitor-visible.  
**Brainstorm prompts:**
- Is salary band select enough, or do founders need free-text equity later?
- When does hiding band destroy trust?

### T5. Success fee / contingency incentives
**Evidence:** Trade press 15–30% contingency; AskTheHeadhunter agency critique; Dover fractional alternative; ILO worker-fee ethics.  
**For Demigod:** **10% on hire, free for talent** sits below classic agency contingency and opposite of worker-paid fees. Incentives: paid only when hire starts → avoid spray résumés.  
**Brainstorm prompts:**
- How to explain 10% vs 20% agencies without sounding cheaper-only?
- What guarantee language is honest (no 90-day replacement theater)?

### T6. Intake / forms / conversion
**Evidence:** HubSpot multi-step; Venture Harbour cases; Typeform conversational UX; Fillout 25-question intake; Demigod forms roadmap 2026-08-15.  
**For Demigod:** Multi-step + match-critical-only fields already shipped in foot WIZ; academic intake lists are *menus*, not requirements.  
**Brainstorm prompts:**
- Which of Fillout’s 25 questions still tempt us and should stay Non-goals?
- Drop-off by step if analytics ever allowed?

### T7. Competitors & category position
**Evidence:** Catalog §D + DEMIGOD-COMPETITOR-DIFF-2026-07-13.  
| Category | Examples | Demigod is NOT |
|----------|----------|----------------|
| Startup job board | Wellfound | Apply-button volume |
| Curated mutual network | Underdog | Closed membership theater without pilots |
| ATS/CRM | Ashby, Gem, Greenhouse | Self-serve seat product |
| Recruiter marketplace | Paraform | Marketplace of recruiters |
| Classic agency | Hunt Club / contingency | 15–25% spray |

**Brainstorm prompts:**
- One sentence: closer to Underdog or to boutique agency—and what we refuse from both?
- When would “Autopilot AI sourcing” language poison trust?

### T8. Privacy, spam, and passive talent
**Evidence:** Privacy compliance blogs; double opt-in intros; Underdog anonymity; developer trust recruiting (daily.dev).  
**For Demigod:** “Not a public profile / no blasts” is a category weapon against LinkedIn cold spam and board scrape culture.  
**Brainstorm prompts:**
- What proof of privacy can we show without fake security theater?
- How to serve passive talent without LinkedIn dependency?

### T9. Match quality & channels
**Evidence:** Meeting-channels research; informal matching clustering; signaling experiments in labor econ.  
**For Demigod:** Channel choice (human intro vs posting vs network) is the product. Informal networks are uneven—curated intros can expand access *or* re-create clique if uncareful.  
**Brainstorm prompts:**
- How do we measure “good match” without SLA clocks?
- Referral partners vs cold brief volume?

### T10. Weak ties & networks (Granovetter → LinkedIn Science)
**Evidence:** Granovetter 1973/1974; Aral et al. *Science* LinkedIn RCT (~20M users)—weaker ties drive more applications and mobility online.  
**For Demigod:** Open social graphs *work* for discovery but also produce spam and uneven access. Demigod’s product is **curated weak ties** (human-selected intros), not “add more LinkedIn edges.”  
**Brainstorm prompts:**
- Are partner/referrals Demigod’s intentional weak-tie machine?
- How to avoid recreating only-strong-tie (clique) pools?

### T11. Outbound firm-driven search vs inbound boards
**Evidence:** “Hunting for talent” (Black/Koning et al.); Finlay & Coverdill headhunters; HN “Who is hiring” ban on agencies.  
**For Demigod:** Boutique human search is **outbound intermediary** work with consent gates—not ATS inbound queue. Community norms (HN) reject agency spam; mutual yes is the trust patch.  
**Brainstorm prompts:**
- What % of pilot delivery should be outbound (potter-sourced) vs inbound brief?
- How do we brand “human hunt” without sounding like spray contingency?

### T12. Regulation: automated decision systems (CA FEHA ADS)
**Evidence:** Strategy research 2026-08-04 on FEHA ADS breadth (tools that screen/rank/recommend can create employer liability even with human final decision).  
**For Demigod:** Research-only caution: **no fitScore / no AI hire verdict** is not only brand—it may reduce product-category risk for CA employers. Not legal advice.  
**Brainstorm prompts:**
- What language must never appear on compare notes?
- When would a “rank” feature become ADS theater?

## Cross-theme tensions (useful for brainstorming)

1. **Scale vs care:** Horton-style recs want volume; Demigod brand wants human care.  
2. **Algorithm lift vs bias risk:** More software → more need for human veto transparency.  
3. **Privacy vs proof:** Talent wants privacy; startups want evidence (outcomes, optional PR/resume).  
4. **Lower fee vs agency perception:** 10% can read “discount” or “aligned”—positioning choice.  
5. **Curated vs open:** Underdog-style selectivity vs growth of talent pool.
