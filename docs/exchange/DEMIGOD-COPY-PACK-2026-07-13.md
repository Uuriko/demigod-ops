# Demigod copy pack — 2026-07-13

Voice: calm, precise, SF founder-grade. Honest > hype. Human judgment first.

## Voice rules
1. Prefer concrete outcomes over adjectives.
2. Never promise speed SLAs (no 48h).
3. Dual doors only: hiring vs job-seeking — never two “hire talent” labels.
4. Pending services: say “pending” not “coming soon!” hype.
5. Fee: “10% only when you hire” once per section max.
