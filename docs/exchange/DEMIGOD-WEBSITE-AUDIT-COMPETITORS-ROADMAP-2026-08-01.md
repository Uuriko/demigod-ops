# Demigod hiring-flow competitor audit

**Observed:** 2026-08-02
**Scope:** direct and adjacent startup hiring products with a public employer or candidate flow
**Decision:** copy familiar calibration, consent, interview, and follow-up patterns; do not copy
marketplace volume, ATS, recruiter-marketplace, trial, or AI-verdict machinery.

## Answer: is “90-day outcome” real?

Yes, but the exact label is niche.

- [Teracrowd](https://teracrowd.com/) publicly asks for “your 90-day outcome.”
- [Gun.io](https://gun.io/resources/2023/02/what-to-prepare-before-hiring-through-gun-io/)
  asks clients to define 30/60/90-day goals as part of role calibration.
- [Greenhouse](https://www.greenhouse.com/blog/hiring-top-talent-playbook) recommends documenting
  ideal 30/60/90 outcomes before a role goes live.
- Direct marketplaces more often say **first deliverables**, **what success looks like**, or
  **what are you building**.
- Other products use 90 days for a replacement guarantee, which is a commercial term, not a role
  definition.

Conclusion: the concept is established. Presenting the exact phrase as a universal intake
convention would be misleading. Demigod should ask for the **first concrete result** and offer a
30–90 day horizon as optional calibration help.

## Representative current landscape

This is a broad representative sweep, not a claim that every recruiting company on earth has a
publicly inspectable flow.

| Product | Public flow | Treatment of 90 days | Demigod decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Wellfound Autopilot](https://wellfound.com/recruit/all-features/autopilot) | Onboard and calibrate → recruiter sources and pitches → employer approves interested candidates → calls are booked | Not an intake headline | Copy plain stages and warm-candidate review; reject volume promises |
| [Underdog.io](https://underdog.io/built-for-hiring-managers) | Define stack, seniority, ownership, compensation, and location → receive opted-in introductions → run interviews → pay on hire | Commercial replacement period, not role framing | Closest overall model: few relevant introductions, salary early, employer owns interview |
| [Candidate Labs](https://www.candidatelabs.com/) | Kickoff with people who know the bar → contextual candidate submissions → tight pass/advance feedback → support through close | No public intake rule | Copy context and one-sentence feedback; do not invent speed claims |
| [Paraform role setup](https://www.paraform.com/help/article/role-setup) | Rank Required / Optional / Deal-breaker criteria and interview stages → specialist recruiter asks permission for the exact role → candidate reconfirms → employer interviews | [90-day replacement](https://www.paraform.com/for-companies), separate from intake | Copy explicit deal-breakers and role-specific consent; reject recruiter-marketplace machinery |
| [Dover Recruiting Marketplace](https://help.dover.com/en/articles/12143011-getting-started-with-the-recruiting-marketplace) | Deposit → compare recruiters → intro calls → select commercial model → recruiter works in a shared ATS | Guarantee varies by recruiter | Keep terms explicit; reject deposits, multiple pricing modes, and shared-ATS bloat |

| [A.Team](https://www.a.team/get-started) | Describe what is being built → advisor scopes the work → meet a small team → interview → builders embed | Project/contractor promises, not permanent-hire calibration | Copy work-first scoping; reject contractor speed promises |

| [Arc](https://arc.dev/how-arc-works) | Share goals, budget, job, and location → meet vetted matches → interview and hire | Three-month rematch is commercial | Copy transparent needs and employer-owned interviews; do not claim “risk-free” |

| [Hunt Club](https://www.huntclub.com/our-tech) | Search strategy → network longlist → warm introductions → human assessment/calibration → references and close | No public 90-day intake rule | Copy warm context and human judgment; reject executive-search weight and timing claims |
| [Riviera Partners](https://www.rivierapartners.com/insights/how-to-structure-a-technology-executive-search-from-kickoff-to-close/) | Align on the business problem and longer success picture → research → evidence-rich shortlist → purpose-specific interviews → references → offer/onboarding | Uses a longer success picture | Copy problem-first calibration and distinct interview purposes for senior roles |
| [Mercor AI interview](https://talent.docs.mercor.com/support/ai-interview) | Candidate profile/application → mandatory role-specific AI interview → evaluation/matching → sometimes a short paid trial | No public 90-day frame | Reject mandatory opaque AI interviews; keep declared software rules and human decisions |
| [Andela](https://help.andela.com/hc/en-us/articles/43074786393875-Contract-key-terms-summary) / [Revelo](https://www.revelo.com/pricing) | Match global contractors → interview → short paid engagement/trial → continue or replace | Detailed outcomes move into engagement/onboarding | Relevant only when contract form and law support paid trials; never unpaid by default |
| [Teracrowd](https://teracrowd.com/) | Define a 90-day outcome, success criteria, and failure point → candidate has an AI conversation → employer receives scores and evidence | Exact phrase is the intake | Confirms the phrase exists; reject score-as-verdict and keep plain first-result wording |

## Cross-market pattern worth copying

The common, useful flow is:

1. Define the problem, role, compensation, work mode, and first observable result.
2. Calibrate must-haves, preferences, deal-breakers, and the interview path.
3. Review a small number of contextual candidates rather than an application pile.
4. Ask the candidate about the exact company and role before sharing identity.
5. Let the startup run the interview and make the decision.
6. Keep one clear next-update date after an intro until the process closes.
7. Charge only under the written commercial terms after a real outcome.

This matches current candidate evidence: [Employ’s 2026 Job Seeker Nation report](https://www.jobvite.com/2026-job-seeker-nation-report/)
identifies silence as a major candidate problem, while
[Ashby’s 2026 recruiting-operations benchmark](https://www.ashbyhq.com/talent-trends-report/reports/recruiting-operations-benchmarks-talent-trends)
shows that slow post-interview decisions hurt candidate sentiment. Demigod needs decision cadence,
not another candidate account or dashboard.

## Demigod flow after the audit

Website release v903 implements the public-language change while preserving stored-data
compatibility:

- stored field remains `90day-outcome` so existing submissions and matching logic do not migrate;
- public question is **“What should this person accomplish first?”**;
- help text says one concrete result is enough and a 30–90 day horizon is useful, not mandatory;
- review labels the answer **First result**;
- public explanation says this is neither an unpaid trial nor a day-90 performance guarantee;
- talent entry says **“Open to the right startup?”** / **“Share what I’d consider”**, not “join a
  network” that Demigod does not operate;
- the public sequence is **define → calibrate/review → role-specific opt-in → interview → hire/pay**.

The private operator flow uses existing records rather than a new portal:

- founder approval exposes the exact candidate-role receipt before candidate consent;
- candidate identity stays private until the exact role is approved;
- mutual consent is bound to the current role/candidate pair;
- an intro receipt must name that pair and store one `nextUpdateAt` checkpoint;
- sample, terminal, and invalid legacy pilots are excluded from open checkpoint counts;
- detailed 30/60/90 onboarding belongs after a real hire.

## Explicit anti-features

Do not add these without new evidence:

- ATS or open application queue;
- candidate portal or another account;
- recruiter marketplace;
- mandatory AI interview or public fit score;
- “top X%,” shortlist-volume, or speed SLA claims;
- trial program for permanent hires;
- replacement guarantee or subscription machinery;
- a separate quality-of-hire score before enough real outcomes exist.

[Greenhouse’s quality-of-hire caution](https://www.greenhouse.com/blog/the-problem-with-measuring-quality-of-hire)
is relevant here: early 30/60/90 checks can measure onboarding and management as much as recruiting.
The honest first metric remains a real accepted brief → reviewed pair → mutual yes → intro → hire.
