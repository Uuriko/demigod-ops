Anti-patterns:

- Multi-model voting or “boss model” deference without evidence, ownership boundaries, and deterministic gates.
- Concurrent agents editing overlapping canonical files—or repeatedly auditing the same stable surface—to appear productive.
- Documentation drift: duplicated rules, future/stale timestamps, contradictory publish authority, or claims that disk/CDN/live match without hashes.
