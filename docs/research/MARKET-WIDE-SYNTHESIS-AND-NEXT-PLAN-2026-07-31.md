## 1. Macro themes (2026 eng hiring)

### 1.1 AI-native bar is mainstream
- Industry consensus: assess **orchestration, verification, judgment** — not syntax-only LeetCode.  
- Companies (Meta, Google pilots, Canva, Sierra) allow or require AI in coding rounds.  
- Aptitude / “validate AI output” assessments surged; pure memorization devalued.  
- Risk: AI-allowed take-homes can look uniformly polished → **debrief / process signal** becomes the differentiator (Promptster thesis; Exceptional debrief).

### 1.2 Application noise is extreme
- Startup ATS inbound is flooded; “best builders stop applying cold” is Builders’ / industry pitch.  
- Warm intros and portable bars (Triplebyte heirs) re-emerge as product categories.

### 1.3 Recruiting economics (public ranges)
| Model | Typical public fee |
|-------|-------------------|
| Traditional contingency agency | **15–25%** first-year salary (often 20%) |
| Paraform-style marketplace | **20–25%** or fixed bounty $10–30k |
| Flat / fractional (Dover etc.) | ~$4–7k per hire or subscription |
| Platform lower contingency | **10–15%** (some platform models) |
| **Builders (public /refer)** | **10% cash (base+bonus)** first hire → **15%** after |
| **Demigod** | **10% base only** (no public escalator) |

**Demigod sits at the cheap, transparent end** of placement fees — if delivery quality holds.

### 1.4 New YC-class competitors (recruiting)
Not exhaustive; category heat:

| Player | Angle |
|--------|--------|
| **Builders / Exceptional** | Proof-of-work + AI take-home cert + multi-co placement (Pensive) |

### 1.5 What “proof of work” means now
Three layers:

1. **Public artifacts** — GitHub, shipped product (Skillsync, portfolios)  
2. **Timed AI-native builds** — Builders, Sierra, agentic OAs  
3. **Process telemetry** — Promptster (how they steered the agent)  

Demigod today: layer 1 via resume/experience + human review. Does **not** own 2–3 as products.

## 4. Strategic implications for Demigod

### Keep (defensible)
- Mutual yes + 10% **base-only** (clearer/cheaper than Builders 10%→15% cash)  
- SF density + map/ATS honesty  
- Human review of work evidence  
- No fake SLA / board theater  

### Do not build
- Take-home IDE / proctoring / agentic OA product  
- Multi-company “pass once skip all screens” promise  

### Absorb from market (lightweight)
- Treat Builders/Exceptional/Sierra-style work as **evidence tier** (docs done)  
- Match review asks orchestration questions (checklist done)  
- Optionally tag roles “AI-native builders preferred” in briefs (ops language only)  

### Competitive white space Demigod can own
1. **SF founder relationship + 90-day outcome** (Builders is multi-co broadcast)  
2. **Honest fee** (base-only 10% vs agency 20% and Builders cash escalator)  
3. **Map-grounded reality** (open roles, aging, neighborhood) — Builders does not own this  
4. **Mutual consent** as brand (vs push-placement)
