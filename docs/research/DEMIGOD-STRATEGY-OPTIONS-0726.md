# Demigod — strategy options (deep research synthesis, 2026-07-26)

> Research options, not operating truth.

Synthesis of 5 parallel research streams (competitors, growth channels, product/monetization, data/
defensibility, market/creative), ~60 sources each, skepticism-flagged. These are **options to weigh**,
not recommendations or positioning claims. Where the research corrected a widely-held belief it's noted.

---

## What all five streams independently converged on

1. **The market is a trust vacuum.** Both sides are miserable (Greenhouse CEO: "trust at an all-time low
   for both job seekers and recruiters"). AI-slop flood: LinkedIn ~11k applications/minute (+45% YoY),
   ~750 apps/offer, ~75% never reach a human. The industry's reflex is *more* AI (outbound spray at
   750M+ scraped profiles) or *more friction* (candidate-as-suspect ID checks). **Nobody owns
   honest + human + local.**
2. **The moat is not data or "honesty."** Per a16z/NfX: data-scale effects are weak (asymptotic,
   scrapable = a cost line). "We're honest" is table-stakes rhetoric every vendor claims. The only things
   that compound: **outcome-labeled matches in ONE atomic SF segment** (mutual-interest, reply/fill/
   retention — a *flow* only Demigod's transactions see) **+ repeat-employer trust.** The directory +
   job-age ledger are an **acquisition wedge, not the moat.**
3. **Job-seekers essentially never pay.** WTP ladder: employers/recruiters > investors (alt-data) >
   GTM teams >> job-seekers (~$0). Money is always *adjacent* to the free data (Levels.fyi, Layoffs.fyi,
   TheOrg all monetized adjacent, not the dataset).
4. **For a solo founder at zero, two engines start cold:** contingency placements (cash) and a free
   attributed data artifact that earns an audience (the Layoffs.fyi arc — cited by press, zero outbound).
5. **Skeptical correction (competitors + market streams):** ghost-*job* prevalence (27–40%) is mostly
   from vendors selling detectors — inflated, and a paid consumer detector is a proven dud (GhostJob
   extension: 23 users, $0). The **better-sourced, government-documented pain is fake *candidates*** —
   and the cheapest defense nobody sells is **being physically, verifiably in SF** (hard for a deepfake/
   remote-fraud operation to fake).

**Corrections to circulating "facts":** Mercor/Micro1 are AI-training-labor businesses now, not recruiters.
Braintrust's talent token collapsed −99.88%. Pallet and Draftboard both abandoned warm-intro/community
hiring platforms. So the "AI recruiter" giants aren't in this lane, and the "warm-intro-as-platform" bet
has failed twice — but the human trusted-*node* still works.

## The options (grouped; each = insight · conviction · effort · honest tradeoff)

### Positioning (how Demigod is framed — pick the frame)
**P1 — "Human, on purpose."** Counter-position directly against the AI-spray doom loop: personally
confirmed real roles, double-opt-in intros, no roster, no black hole. · *High conviction* (all 5 streams;
uncontested after Braintrust poisoned the "no-roster" angle) · Zero build, it's copy · Tradeoff: caps at
solo/small scale — but Triplebyte's "break-even, sometimes profitable" (a VC disappointment) is a *solo
win condition*, not a flaw.
**P2 — Geography/IRL as the trust mechanism.** "SF, in-person-verifiable" as the answer to the fake-
candidate/deepfake wave that Persona/CLEAR fight with friction. Verifying *presence* needs no résumé-PII.
· *High conviction, most novel finding* · Low build (it's a policy + a check) · Tradeoff: shrinks the
market to genuinely-SF people — which is the point.
**P3 — The wedge is the MODEL, not the 10%.** Others already do flat 10%/$4,900; on a $180k SF salary
10% is still ~$18k. Lead with talent-free + mutual-consent + human-reviewed. · *High conviction* · Copy
only · Tradeoff: none — this is just not mis-pricing the pitch.

### Product / build (what to make — all consume assets Demigod already has)
**B1 — Free attributed "who's *really* hiring in SF" data artifact** (from the job-age ledger). The
Layoffs.fyi playbook: one honest, free-to-cite, attributed SF hiring dataset → press/newsletters pull it,
becomes the credibility + inbound flywheel. · *High conviction as an audience engine* (4 of 5 streams) ·
**Low effort — the ledger already computes it** · Tradeoff: frame it as "who's really hiring" (positive,
defensible), NOT a "ghost-job detector" (inflated vendor narrative, crowded with free tools, $0 WTP).
The *empty intersection* the research found: nobody gives candidates open, attributed, company-level
hiring signal — it's all sold to PE.
**B2 — Outcome-loop instrumentation in ONE atomic SF segment** (e.g. seed/A founding engineers). Capture
mutual-interest, reply rates, interview→offer, fill/no-fill, and the crown jewels — rejection reasons +
90-day retention. · *Highest conviction for defensibility* — the one thing that compounds, at low n
(dozens–low-hundreds if concentrated) · Medium (instrumentation + liquidity, not data acquisition) ·
Tradeoff: needs real matches flowing to generate data; inert until then. It's the *moat the rest feeds.*
**B3 — SF hiring-signal feed/API for VCs & recruiters** (the same ledger data, different customer).
"Which SF startups are hiring for role X, how urgently" — proven whitespace (only a feature inside big
platforms; Harmonic/Specter sell exactly this to VCs; Vendr real ACVs: LinkedIn $38k, Crunchbase $20k). ·
*Highest revenue ceiling* · Medium-high (needs a B2B buyer motion even if self-serve) · Tradeoff: a real
customer/sales pivot to validate, not a weekend build.

### Business model (how money works — not mutually exclusive)
**M1 — Contingency stays the cash engine.** 10%-on-hire; raise *volume*, not rate. · Funds everything;
no build · Tradeoff: services income, lumpy, hours-capped — but it's the only thing that pays *now*.
**M2 — Monetize adjacent, never the job-seeker.** Sell hiring signals to VCs/recruiters (B3) or newsletter
sponsors (HR/startups is the top-value newsletter niche: ~$35–43 per 1k subs/slot). · Tradeoff: all of
these need the audience (B1) to exist first.

### Segment (where to aim)
**S1 — Own "the single permanent seed-stage SF hire."** The lane between fractional (Toptal/A.Team),
recruiter-bounties (Paraform $10–30k), and free volume boards (Wellfound/YC) is empty. "SF founder needs
their first 10 people, locally, this quarter." · *High conviction — the orphaned segment* · Tradeoff:
seed hiring runs on networks, so supply-sourcing is the hard part (the retro/codex thread).

## What the research says NOT to do
- A paid consumer ghost-job detector (crowded free tools, $0 WTP).
- A two-sided marketplace or ATS SaaS (funded businesses, wrong at zero).
- Retained search (needs decades of reputation).
- Reselling raw scraped people-data (commoditized + PII-risky).
- A weekly Ramp-style economist newsroom (credential-gated, 12-month payback).
- Near-term programmatic SEO (per-company "is X hiring" search volume is *unconfirmed* — verify before
  betting).
- "Platformize warm intros" (Pallet + Draftboard both failed at it — be the *human node*, not the platform).

## The through-line the streams agree on
Positioning (P1–P3) + a free honest SF hiring dataset (B1) are the cheap, on-value acquisition/credibility
engine. Their real job is to generate the two things that actually compound: **outcome data in one
segment (B2) and repeat-employer trust.** Money comes from contingency now (M1) and, later, selling
signal to VCs/recruiters (B3) — never from job-seekers.

## Caveats on the research (be skeptical before external use)
- Nearly all ARR/user/valuation figures are self-reported/unaudited — directional only.
- Ghost-job prevalence stats vary by denominator (% postings vs % employers vs % filled) — cite the
  source, don't blend; and they skew high (detector vendors).
- The ghost-job *disclosure laws* (Ontario Jan-2026; NY/NJ/CA bills) appeared with conflicting sourcing
  across streams — **confirm any specific law before relying on it.**
- Web-search budget was exhausted mid-run on 3 streams; a few secondary numbers (H-1B dynamics, exact
  Reddit/SEO conversion figures, some 2026 funding) are thin and flagged in the raw agent reports
  (`/tmp/.../scratchpad/research-*.md`).
