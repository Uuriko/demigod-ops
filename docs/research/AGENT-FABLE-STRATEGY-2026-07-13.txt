**Demigod GTM Strategy — Fable, 2026-07-12**

## 1) 90-Day Strategy: Earn Conversations, Prove One Match

Recruiting is many-to-many matchmaking (a16z): the system's job is to earn the human conversation, never to spam it. Demigod's edge is the *mutual-yes*: Kim/Pergler (SMJ 2025) shows firm-driven search fills roles but raises turnover when candidates didn't choose the fit — our 90-day-outcome + candidate opt-in is the direct academic counter, and it becomes the pitch.

- **Days 1–30 — Demand.** 15+ warm SF founder DMs (honest pre-seed framing, zero delivery claims). Convert 2–3 into logged pilots. Require MOC-style role clarity (mission/outcomes/competencies) as the intake bar — it filters unserious demand and *is* the matching signal.
- **Days 31–60 — Deliver.** One white-glove match end-to-end: sourced, mutual-yes, intro, 90-day outcome logged. Move fast and hard through process (a16z founding-hire). This single real receipt replaces all seed data.
- **Days 61–90 — Proof loop.** Publish the receipt (with permission), 3 founder testimonials, repeat at 3–5 concurrent roles. Site changes stay minimal; proof assets carry GTM.

## 2) Monetization

Market contingency fees run 15–25%, 20% common. Demigod at **10% on hire** is a credible half-price wedge justified by lower-touch, higher-signal matching. Path: free/white-glove pilot (now) → 10% success fee on first real hires (Stripe pending; invoice manually if needed — don't block revenue on integration) → later, retained tier for repeat founders once ≥3 receipts exist. No fee experiments until the first paid hire clears.

Demand first. Everything else waits behind a real conversation.
