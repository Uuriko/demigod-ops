## 1. Research rules we keep (2026, public-source only)

These are honesty rules, not a Clay feature list.

1. Domain-first identity. Never map a company to an ATS board by name.
2. Two clocks: `firstObservedAt` is ours; `postedAt` / `updated_at` are the employer's. Never impute one from the other.
3. Snapshot date on every public directory claim. A count without `as of` is a lie.
4. Empty and thin rows stay empty. `unknown` beats a guessed website, stack, or stage.
5. Requisition IDs are a trap when one board uses one id for many jobs. Abstain (ONE/MULTI) rather than count distinct reqs as truth.
6. Public-source waterfall only: first-party site → YC/Wikidata/HN → public ATS JSON. No people-data providers.
7. Under-claim. A missed board is better than attributing another company's jobs.
8. Directory is a browse surface, not matching inventory. Sample PM/Designer/Growth cards stay dead.
9. Refresh cadence is an operations fact (`directory-refresh` + timers), not a marketing SLA.
10. Publish is a separate act from refresh. Disk can be fresh while CDN is stale.

## 7. 2026 research keepers (full brief: [research/2026-08-14-practices.md](research/2026-08-14-practices.md))

Steal honesty, not Clay:

1. Three clocks, never one — employer `postedAt` ≠ observer `firstObservedAt` ≠ `lastSeen` / `openRolesAt`.
2. Only a complete successful fetch may close or zero a board (mass-expiry guard).
3. `daysOpen` / velocity stay absent until `firstSeen > board_first_seen`.
4. Ledger PK is `(provider, board_token, job_post_id)` — never `requisition_id`.
5. Parse ATS dates as defined (Lever epoch ms; Ashby `publishedAt` = last published; GH `updated_at` is an edit clock).
6. Domain is the company key; name is a label. Split homonyms.
7. Self-report is not inventory (YC “hiring” ≠ `openRoles`).
8. Snapshot date belongs on the surface (generated-at ≠ CDN-published-at ≠ last-ATS-poll-at).
9. Public-source waterfall only. Stop at first confident result.
10. Empty/uncertain enrichment never overwrites a verified field.
11. Null, don’t invent.
12. Canary the parsers (known GH/Lever/Ashby boards + schema version).

Do not copy: people-data waterfalls, recipe DSL, credit meters, auto-DM, inferred facts, coverage theater.
