# DIE similar-product scan — 2026-08-15

**Question:** which mechanisms from adjacent recruiting, relationship-intelligence, and data
products would make Demigod materially more useful, and how should DIE implement them?

**Method:** primary product pages and official documentation only. Vendor claims are treated as
product evidence, not independent proof of accuracy or hiring validity. This scan extracts
mechanisms; it does not copy vendor positioning, data rights, or automation boundaries.

## Executive conclusion

The common winning unit is a **role workspace**, not a generic AI agent and not a table by itself.
It starts with an accepted brief and calibration rubric, then joins company context, all permitted
candidate channels, prior relationship history, evidence-backed review, a small active shortlist,
structured interviews, and controlled action.

DIE already owns most of these primitives. The fastest strong product is to compose them around one
role, preserve their authority boundaries, and only then generalize the table/run infrastructure.

The market also supports a second conclusion: rediscovery and relationship context should come
before expensive net-new sourcing. Several products explicitly unify ATS/CRM history with new
search, suppress recent contacts, and refresh stale profiles. Demigod should start with consented
submissions, owned touch history, referrals, and existing pairs; external person data is a later,
rights-dependent channel rather than the foundation.

## Products and mechanisms worth taking

### Clay — programmable data workbench

Official sources:

- [account research agents](https://university.clay.com/docs/account-research-agents)
- [functions](https://university.clay.com/docs/functions)
- [audiences](https://university.clay.com/docs/audiences)
- [search](https://university.clay.com/docs/search)
- [data waterfalls](https://university.clay.com/docs/building-a-data-waterfall)
- [signals](https://university.clay.com/docs/signals)

Take:

- typed row/packet workbench with visible evidence and unknowns;
- reusable, versioned functions rather than copied column recipes;
- first-acceptable-result waterfalls with provider traces;
- sample-first runs, budgets, archives, and explicit writeback review;
- persistent entity memory, material-change signals, and dynamic segments;
- natural-language search compiled to a visible filter.

Adapt for Demigod:

- companies, roles, candidates, pairs, and outcomes are different trust zones;
- evidence and action authority remain separate objects;
- cost is measured per useful fact or completed review, not presented as a credit game.

## Product decisions

### Build now

1. **Role workspace:** accepted-role truth, calibrated role packet, company packet, candidate channels,
   capped shortlist, review evidence, interview plan, relationship paths, calls, and outcomes in one
   private surface.
2. **Rediscovery first:** consented profiles, owned touches, prior pairs, referrals, suppression, and
   stale-profile flags before paid net-new person data.
3. **Evidence questions:** filters narrow the pool; explicit role questions retrieve evidence and
   show unknown/conflict states.
4. **Structured interviews:** criteria-to-question plan, evidence notes, disagreement, and debrief.
5. **Dynamic pools:** visible deterministic filters, membership changes, and no implicit action.
6. **Controlled action:** draft → exact preview → authorization receipt → execute → reconcile.
7. **Funnel learning:** channel yield, evidence usefulness, corrections, stage duration, and outcome
   links without causal overclaim.

### Build after proof or rights

- licensed external candidate search;
- transcript capture and summarization;
- ATS/CRM write adapters;
- paid company/person provider integrations;
- multi-tenant RBAC and warehouse sync;
- natural-language workflow authoring.

### Deliberately avoid

- global candidate fit scores or autonomous hiring decisions;
- automated AI interviews as an early product dependency;
- contact scraping, inferred protected traits, or unlicensed profile copying;
- autonomous outreach or sequence continuation without per-action authority;
- vendor marketplace, credit theater, or a graph/database rewrite before measured need;
- merging people or companies on names alone.

## Differentiation

Demigod should not try to beat every vendor at its own horizontal product. Its wedge is the joined
system those products usually split apart:

```text
public company and hiring truth
  + accepted founder brief and calibrated rubric
  + consented candidate and relationship memory
  + evidence-backed human review
  + mutual-consent intro authority
  + outcome-linked learning
```

That system is useful before large-scale data acquisition, and each later provider or model must
demonstrably improve one part of it.
