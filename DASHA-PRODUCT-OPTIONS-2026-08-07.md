# Dasha product options

Updated: 2026-08-07

## Boundary

The Thesis Card, receipts, forecasts, prediction rounds and every renamed descendant are permanently scrapped. They are not candidates below. `$dasha` also does not acquire invented utility: no holder gate, yield, governance promise, reward scheme or implied endorsement.

The observed product assets are narrower: a distinctive landing page, a browser-based Studio that exports post/story/banner images, editable fragment links, a verified mint handoff and a small open-source Desk. There is not yet evidence of repeat use, real remix chains or willingness to pay.

## The product question

Dasha needs to own a repeatable behavior, not another page. The weakest sufficient product hypothesis is:

> People will pass a finished cultural artifact onward more often when the artifact is immediately useful as an image and remains one-click editable for the next person.

This hypothesis supports a concrete first product without assuming a new social network, marketplace, token economy, protocol or creator business.

## Option map

| Option | What a person does | Why it could matter | Main risk | Cheapest real test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Remix Relay** | Opens a finished post, changes one thing, exports it and passes the editable child onward | A small behavior the current Studio can already support; unlike a blank meme generator, the handoff is the product | People may keep only the PNG and discard editability | Ten real one-to-one handoffs; pass only if at least two create a non-operator second generation |
| **2. Moment Capsules** | Adds one remix to a temporary group zine for a cultural moment | Turns isolated posts into a collective artifact without building a feed | Operator may do all the assembly | Distribute three editable Capsules; pass only if a non-operator reopens and changes one |
| **3. Live Remix Room** | Joins a timed prompt, makes one artifact and sees the set assembled at the end | An event can create urgency and community without financial pressure | It may be fun once but not repeat | Run manually with the existing Studio; require repeat attendance before real-time code |
| **4. Culture Kit** | A community forks a coherent set of templates, colors and starter lines for its own moment | Converts Dasha from one coin's meme tool into reusable culture infrastructure | Generic brand-kit and AI creative tools are crowded | Build one kit manually for one outside community; pass only if they reuse it without operator help |
| **5. Open Creative Brief** | Forks a licensed starter into an editable submission for a real community brief | The editable artifact could make creative bounties faster and rights clearer | Easily collapses into an ordinary bounty board | One manual paid brief on existing communication/payment rails; require three usable forks and a repeat buyer |
| **6. In-feed Remix** | Opens, edits and returns a remix without leaving Farcaster | Feed discovery, signed-in social context and sharing are native distribution advantages | A wrapper cannot rescue an unwanted core behavior | Build only after Relay works; then reuse the current Studio inside the thinnest Mini App shell |
| **7. Culture Package** | A community distributes one portable bundle of source links, creative assets, editable recipes and explicit reuse terms | A plausible B2B pivot: people and agents get the same bounded cultural source material without joining another platform | Becomes a branded ZIP/CMS or makes unsupported “official” claims unless outside reuse and control evidence exist | Deliver one static package with no registry or platform code; pass only if an outside community publishes from it and reuses an editable recipe uncoached |
| **8. Lore Vault** | Enters a contract and reads sourced origin, mint evidence, disputed claims, cultural mutations and current links without scores or recommendations | Fills a possible gap between market intelligence and ordinary meme history; Dasha already has one manual evidence ledger | Episodic demand, editorial cost, defamation/likeness/copyright risk and easy confusion with a buy signal | Build at most three static source pages manually; pass only on unprompted return use and non-trading decisions |
| **9. Transaction Preflight** | Pastes an unsigned Solana request and gets a plain-language action/risk preview | Solves a concrete crypto trust problem unrelated to meme creation | Security-heavy, crowded and incompatible with Dasha's cultural identity | Separate landing test plus real unsigned requests; never bolt it onto Studio |

## Ranking

1. **Remix Relay** — strongest immediate product because it uses what is already built and makes a distinct, falsifiable promise.
2. **Moment Capsules** — best adjacent community product if group contribution outperforms one-to-one handoffs.
3. **Culture Packages** — strongest business pivot if outside communities repeatedly want the source material and editable system rather than only a Dasha artifact.
4. **Live Remix Rooms** — promising operating format, but manual events should precede software.
5. **Open Creative Briefs** — potentially valuable after editable handoffs and reuse terms are proven.
6. **Lore Vault** — a manual trust/research fallback if Relay fails, not a multi-token platform hypothesis.
7. **In-feed Remix** — a distribution layer, not a product hypothesis.
8. **Transaction Preflight** — the cleanest truly different pivot, but it should become a separate product rather than distort Dasha.

## Decision now

Keep the public product legible as **Dasha Studio: start from a finished signal, change one thing, pass it on**. Internally, call the behavior test **Remix Relay**. Do not market an Open Culture Objects platform or protocol yet; the current link format is only a Dasha project format until an independent renderer and real multi-generation use exist.

The next build is therefore not accounts, profiles, a gallery, a token gate, a Discord bot, AI generation, contests, voting, rewards, royalties or an onchain registry. The existing Studio and Relay instrument are already sufficient to test the key behavior. More software before that result would make the product larger without making the hypothesis more likely to be true.

Culture Package is also not the next file to build. The repository has no root reuse license for Studio looks, seed lines or marketing copy, and no outside recipient has requested a package. A manifest without reuse terms would duplicate Home and Desk; a ZIP with assets would make an unsupported rights decision. The readiness and reversal gates live in [`DASHA-OPEN-CULTURE-OBJECTS.md`](DASHA-OPEN-CULTURE-OBJECTS.md).

## What would change the decision

- Repeated one-to-one remix chains earn a lineage view and portable object fixtures.
- Group participation beating individual handoffs earns Capsules.
- An outside community reusing a package earns a multi-package index; one package does not justify a registry.
- A real buyer requesting a second editable brief earns the creative-work branch.
- Consistent image-only behavior kills protocol work and redirects the Studio toward fast finished assets.
- Failed cultural creation plus repeated demand for contract/source context permits the bounded Lore Vault test below.
- Failure of the cultural loop plus repeated real demand for transaction interpretation permits a clean full pivot to Preflight.

## Bounded fallback — Lore Vault

Current narrative products such as [Kaito](https://docs.kaito.ai/what-is-kaito), [Ruma](https://www.ruma.fun/), [Sharpe](https://www.sharpe.ai/products/narratives) and [Attentra](https://www.attentra.net/) optimize attention, sentiment or momentum. [Bubblemaps](https://wiki.bubblemaps.io/) explains wallet relationships and onchain anomalies. [MemeChain](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22185) demonstrates that onchain identifiers can be joined with websites, imagery and linked social artifacts for forensic research. The possible remaining job is narrower: contract-keyed cultural genealogy with explicit sources and disputes, not another signal terminal.

Do not build a CultureGraph platform. If Relay fails, manually assemble three static vaults: Dasha, one established unrelated meme coin and one noisy token with clone risk. Cap outside-token research at four hours each. Include mint evidence, originating culture, phrase/visual mutations, material artifacts, current links and disputed claims; exclude sentiment, prices, predictions, safety scores and buy recommendations.

Pass only if at least three non-operators return unprompted on another day or request another contract, at least two use a vault for a non-trading decision, and readers do not interpret it as safety or endorsement. Fail on one-time “cool wiki” reactions, demand only for price signals, inability to research two outside tokens honestly within eight hours, or trust/legal ambiguity that cannot be resolved with sourcing and labels. A failure archives the idea; it does not justify scrapers, crowdsourcing or AI-generated lore.

## Current market contrast

[Zora](https://support.zora.co/en/articles/4641857) makes posts and creator profiles tradeable and adds creator-generation tooling. [Farcaster Mini Apps](https://miniapps.farcaster.xyz/) provide feed distribution, identity, notifications and wallet rails. Those products support monetization and distribution; neither result proves that people want Dasha's editable relay behavior. Dasha should test that missing behavior directly instead of copying their infrastructure.
