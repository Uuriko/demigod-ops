## Executive conclusion

Crypto tooling is crowded at every layer from token creation through execution and P&L. The relatively open job is narrower: preserve what someone claimed, why, with what confidence, and what would prove it wrong **before** the market outcome; then resolve the claim without deleting misses.

~~Dasha should become a lightweight reasoning-and-receipts layer between discovery and execution.~~ **(Superseded 2026-08-08 — product is culture production + honest mint rails + Lobby/Simp, not receipts.)** It should link to incumbent data and trading products instead of rebuilding them. Dasha is the cultural sponsor and first case study, not a required asset, an implied celebrity endorsement, or an Ansem product.

## Primary evidence

### Creation and creator coins

- [Pump fee documentation](https://pump.fun/docs/fees) shows that creation, creator fees and graduated trading are native platform mechanics.
- [Pump bonding-curve documentation](https://pump.fun/docs/bonding-curve) describes immediate price discovery and automatic graduation.
- [Zora Creator Coins](https://support.zora.co/en/articles/6316801) makes every profile a coin, with creator vesting and trading revenue.

Conclusion: creator-token infrastructure is not Dasha's product opening. It is also the highest association, promotion and reputation-risk surface.

### Attention markets

- [Kaito Yaps](https://docs.kaito.ai/kaito-yaps-tokenized-attention) measures proof-of-work, reputation-weighted engagement and perceived insight.
- [Kaito's open protocol](https://docs.kaito.ai/kaito-yaps-tokenized-attention/yaps-open-protocol) makes attention data usable by other products.

Conclusion: a generic Dasha attention leaderboard would compete with better-capitalized networks and invite farming. Dasha can make artifacts people want to discuss without trying to own attention measurement.

### Discovery, verification and risk

- [Solana's verification guide](https://solana.com/pl/developers/guides/getstarted/how-to-verify-a-token) explicitly distinguishes canonical identity from endorsement, value, safety and legitimacy.
- [Jupiter](https://docs.jup.ag/) spans swaps, token pages, discovery, wallets, perps and prediction markets.
- [Birdeye holder APIs](https://docs.birdeye.so/reference/get-token-v1-holder-profile) expose detailed holder classifications and positions.

Conclusion: Dasha should show discrete evidence and unknowns, then send users to authoritative sources. It should not issue a composite risk score.

### Execution and public performance

- [Bullpen](https://bullpen.fi/) markets public performance, following traders, leaderboards, one-tap X sharing and points for sharing alpha.
- [Bullpen social leaderboard documentation](https://docs.bullpen.fi/features/social-leaderboard-and-competitions) confirms competition is a core product layer.

Conclusion: executed-position proof and P&L status already have a natural home. Dasha's non-overlap is reasoning recorded before outcome, including claims not attached to a wallet.

### Forecasting and track records

- [Metaculus track records](https://www.metaculus.com/questions/track-record/) demonstrate calibration curves, scores and complete forecast history.
- [Practical calibration-scoring research](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.07501) argues that useful prediction training needs usability considerations beyond mathematical properness.
- [RFC 3161](https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/rfc3161/) and [OpenTimestamps](https://opentimestamps.org/) show established ways to prove data existed before a time. A browser-generated clock and hash do not provide that proof.

Conclusion: later Dasha history can borrow append-only records, explicit resolution rules and calibration. It should not claim permanence until it uses a real server or trusted timestamp mechanism.

### X distribution

- [X Web Intents](https://docs.x.com/x-for-websites/web-intents/overview) allow user-authorized prefilled posts without application credentials.
- X requires the author to see and choose the final action. Dasha should not auto-post, scrape users into a call feed or manufacture endorsements.

Conclusion: **(updated 2026-08-08)** portable culture artifacts (Studio/share intents, board row, quiz card) plus a stable product URL are the distribution primitive — not receipt URLs. X remains acquisition; Lobby is the controlled on-site chat surface. Discord/TG are not product HQ.

## Dasha and Ansem ecosystem boundaries

Ansem's ecosystem already includes public trading/performance through Bullpen, media through Market Bubble, and community infrastructure. Dasha must not claim affiliation, scrape his identity into product marketing, or require his promotion to function.

The useful adjacency is conceptual: Ansem has publicly discussed high-conviction theses and clear invalidations, while public crypto status artifacts mostly emphasize positions and outcomes. Dasha can turn the missing pre-outcome reasoning into a creator-neutral format usable by any trader, host or community.

## Relatively open jobs

These are hypotheses, not proven demand:

1. Prove a claim existed before the move without exposing a wallet or position size.
2. Preserve the thesis, confidence, horizon and invalidation that existed at commitment time.
3. Resolve the claim against an explicit, contestable rule without deleting misses.
4. Let readers fork a market prompt into an opposing thesis.
5. Turn a stream or Space into a small creator-neutral forecast round and later recap it.
6. Preserve the evidence visible at creation time without calling the asset safe.

## Strategic fit

Weakest sufficient hypothesis: people in public crypto markets already share status artifacts, and some subset may prefer a pre-outcome evidence artifact that preserves optional privacy. This does **not** assume they want moral accountability, a new journal, a Dasha token utility, or another community server.

Cheapest discriminating test: show a working, sub-45-second Thesis Card flow and three credible example cards—including a miss—to at least 30 target users. Measure comprehension, voluntary share intent and repeat creation before building persistence.
