What you actually believe, in your words. Not a target, not a chart pattern — the reason.

02The invalidation

What would make you wrong. This is the field most people skip, and the only one that makes the rest mean anything.

By when. A call without a clock can never be settled, so it never costs anything to have made.

Three examples

The invalidation is the hard field. These are what one looks like when it is doing its job — including the third, which fired.

Example 01Betting on something holding

Thesis

The team ships in public and the commit history backs it up. I think that keeps holding once attention moves on.

Invalid if

Two weeks pass with no public commits and no explanation.

Example 02Betting against the story

Thesis

Most of the volume looks like the same few wallets trading with each other. I do not think the interest is real.

Invalid if

Holder count rises for three straight weeks with no airdrop behind it.

Example 03One that was wrongInvalidated

Thesis

Liquidity depth should improve once the listing lands. That is the only reason I am here.

Invalid if

Depth is still below where it started seven days after listing.

Day seven: depth was lower than at the start. The condition fired. The call was settled, not rewritten.

What the card proves, and what it does not

The limits used to live in small type under the output. They belong here, at the same size as everything else.

Proves

  • The full checksum lets someone compare an exact copy of the same card text. Edit one field and the checksum changes.
  • The card displays the invalidation condition you entered alongside the thesis.

Does not prove

  • When you wrote it. The timestamp comes from your own device and nobody verified it. This is not evidence that you called it first.
  • That the token is safe, real, or worth anything. The tool never looks at it.
  • That you are who you say you are. There is no identity check of any kind.
  • That anyone owes you anything. A card is a record, not a contract.

What this is not

Written plainly, because a tool about accountability should be accountable about itself.

  • No wallet connection. There is nothing to approve and nothing to sign.
  • No trading, no routing, no fees.
  • No safety score. Solana's own guidance is explicit that verification is not endorsement, so we show evidence and let you look.
  • No price prediction and no signal. The tool never has an opinion about your call.
  • Not financial advice, and not a record anyone else is obliged to honour.
  • A token address links out to Solscan so you can inspect it yourself. We never guess an address.
